We performed a controlled cold-start screening experiment to separate deterministic seed prediction, MC averaging, and an uncertainty bonus. ACO was fixed to the revised baseline scheduler with global evaporation and hierarchical feedback. The four conditions were no MLP, deterministic MLP inference, eight-pass MC averaging (lambda=0), and eight-pass MC inference with an uncertainty bonus (lambda=1). Training retained Dropout in all MLP conditions; the deterministic model was exported with Dropout inactive.

An unrestricted 45-second calibration produced only one training example and no model-based decisions. To exercise the mechanisms in a short experiment, all four conditions used the same hard cap of 64 executions per havoc stage, including dynamic expansion. Actor warm-up was 1,000 executions, Learner warm-up was ten batch updates, and model publication/check intervals were one second. These screening settings differ from the default configuration and should not be presented as an evaluation of the original long-run setup.

Each condition was evaluated in three 60-second runs on libpng 1.6.43 and zlib 1.3.1, with a common CPU budget shared by the actor, learner and Redis, identical seed corpora, and randomized run order. All 24 runs completed normally. All 18 MLP runs adopted models and performed actual predictions, with no inference errors; forward-call counts matched one pass for deterministic inference and eight passes for MC inference. Independent gcov replay of final queue corpora yielded median source-branch counts of 407/435/444/448 for zlib and 908/907/904/905 for libpng, in the order listed above.

The uncertainty bonus showed a small positive median difference relative to MC averaging on both targets (four and one branches), but overall MLP gains were not consistent across targets. None of the six incremental exact permutation comparisons was significant after Holm correction (minimum adjusted p=0.6). With three runs per condition, the minimum attainable two-sided exact p-value without ties is 0.1, so this design provides exploratory screening rather than a confirmatory significance test.

Median training-set sizes per run were only 22-29 examples, including 3-4 negative examples. Therefore these measurements do not evaluate a mature online classifier and cannot establish an optimal mechanism or parameter configuration. A matched random-filtering control and a shared, sufficiently trained checkpoint are required to separate learned guidance from generic stochastic filtering. We consequently retained the candidate settings and did not initiate a T/lambda parameter search.
